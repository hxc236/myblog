# Personal Portfolio Site

This context describes the public personal site used to present the site owner's privacy-safe introduction, selected work, writing, and contact links to visitors.

## Language

**Personal Site**:
The public website that brings together the site owner's public introduction, selected projects, blog posts, and contact links.
_Avoid_: Blog, portal, platform

**Site Owner**:
The single person whose identity, work, and writing the Personal Site presents and who controls published content.
_Avoid_: User, administrator, account

**Visitor**:
An anonymous reader who views the public Personal Site without signing in.
_Avoid_: User, customer

**Public Introduction**:
A privacy-safe summary of the Site Owner's technical focus, skills, and technology stack. It excludes a personal profile, location, availability, résumé, portrait, and other private facts.
_Avoid_: Profile, About, account, user information

**Project**:
A curated piece of work selected by the Site Owner for public presentation, with a summary and optional links or images.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, product, case

**Blog Post**:
A piece of long-form writing authored by the Site Owner. A Blog Post may exist as a Draft and may expose a Published Revision to Visitors.
_Avoid_: News, status, article record

**Draft**:
The private working version of a Blog Post that only the Site Owner can view and edit.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, temporary post

**Published Revision**:
The specific version of a Blog Post that Visitors can read, independent of later Draft changes.
_Avoid_: Current draft, live draft

**Archived Post**:
A previously published Blog Post that is no longer public but remains available to the Site Owner for restoration as a Draft.
_Avoid_: Deleted post, hidden draft

**Tag**:
A reusable topic label assigned to Blog Posts so Visitors can discover related writing.
_Avoid_: Keyword, category

**Category**:
The single broad editorial section to which a published Blog Post belongs.
_Avoid_: Tag, topic, collection

**Uncategorized Category**:
The protected fallback Category assigned to Blog Posts when their previous Category is deleted.
_Avoid_: Null category, deleted category, default tag

**Page View**:
An anonymous occurrence of a Visitor opening a Published Revision, retained only for aggregate traffic statistics.
_Avoid_: Browsing record, personal activity, user history

**Reading History**:
A Visitor's device-local list of previously opened Published Revisions; it is not sent to or synchronized by the Personal Site.
_Avoid_: Server history, account history, Page View

**Media Asset**:
An image owned by the Site Owner that may be embedded in a Draft or Published Revision.
_Avoid_: Attachment, database blob, upload record

**Admin Console**:
A private second-phase interface through which the Site Owner will manage Public Introduction, Project, Blog Post, and contact content.
_Avoid_: User center, dashboard, backend
